## 5. Deviations from the design, and why

1. **The approved Guardrails text is not in the repository.** H-060 approved "the Guardrails and in-app copy" and H-056's status line points to them "below", but neither design file nor DECISIONS holds that landing text (only the in-app strings of H-056 §4, §13 and §14 and H-063's clock sentence). Per AGENTS.md §1 the difference is recorded here: the two revised rules ("The AI acts only when you ask.", "Only a person publishes.", with their bodies) are this slice's wording from the decisions' terms, shown only with the flag on, and PROPOSED for the owner's look. The in-app strings follow H-056 where it gives them ("Let the AI act on it", "Let the AI test these", "The AI is already testing ideas: watch it", "Saved by the AI", "Started by the AI", "Done by the AI", the draft Callout, "Stop the AI") and H-063's clock sentence exactly.
2. **Labels show whatever the flag.** "Everything is hidden unless NEXT_PUBLIC_GEMINUS_INVESTIGATIONS=1" is read as the entry points, the page, the links to it and the landing; a row the server labels as the AI's is always shown as the AI's (H-056: every AI-made scenario, run and draft edit is labelled), and no such row exists until the API's flag is on.
3. **"Run by the AI for <person>"** reads "Started by: The AI, for you" or "for the person who asked": the API gives the requester's user ID, not a name.
4. **The step list is the ledger** (A2's `steps[]`), not H-056's candidate-derived list; the H-056 InvestigationDriver and `?investigation=` on results pages are gone (H-061 §2).
5. **The objective's label is "Aim"**, and its four choices are worded as aims ("Lower median lead time", …); the direction is the server's, as A2's `InvestigationObjective.better`.
6. **The photo button is hidden while "Let the AI act on it" is ticked** (H-056 §17: no photo inside an investigation); unticking brings it back.
7. **One link per place (H-027) over the design's "links to what it made" and "links to the existing results page".** H-056 §14 gives both the step list and the candidate table links; the simplicity rule refuses two visible links to one place. The table keeps the results link (the design's own), a step links its proposal, scenario and explanation, a run's results only when no candidate lists that run, and a candidate's proposal state is a link only when no step links it. The model is never linked: the rail's Canvas is the way to the draft, as on the model's list pages.
8. **Stop needs the signed-in user's ID** for an editor: `Auth.userId` is new (from `/me`, which the bootstrap already loads). An editor whose ID is not known yet is not offered Stop; an owner always is. The API decides (403 for another editor).

### R1.4 Deviations from the design, and why

1. **The significance clause stays reworded.** H-056 §4's example says "Geminus compares the runs' means and does not test whether the difference is significant." The default aim is the median lead time, which SIMULATION_SPEC §5 defines as a quantile of the entity cohort pooled across replications (and the 95th percentile likewise), not a mean; "the runs' means" would be false for two of the four aims, the default among them. The page keeps "Geminus compares the runs as they are and does not test whether a difference is significant." (zh-Hant 「Geminus 只比較執行結果本身，不檢定差異是否顯著。」), PROPOSED for the owner.
2. **The closing line's shape is H-056's, adapted to H-061's contract.** H-056 §4: "Lowest median lead time: '<name>', 6.1% lower than the baseline." H-061's closing line carries the candidate's index, not a name (the candidate's words are the AI's idea, which never enters the server's verdict), and the server's signed figures: "Lowest median lead time, by Geminus's comparison: what-if 1, −2.6 min (−6.1%) against the baseline." The "by Geminus's comparison" keeps the line apart from the AI's claim beside it.
3. **The version fact is a label and a value.** The header band's facts are pairs (DESIGN.md Layout), so H-056 §13's "Includes a change the AI wrote" is the value and the version names it: "Version 3: Includes a change the AI wrote".
4. **The phone layout keeps the heads once, above the rows**, as the results' metrics table (H-039), rather than a label in every cell: one layout for the two comparison tables; the candidates are at most three rows.
5. **The Stop question closes by itself when the investigation ends.** Nothing is left to stop; the announcement "The investigation has ended." says why it went, and the focus lands on the outcome.
