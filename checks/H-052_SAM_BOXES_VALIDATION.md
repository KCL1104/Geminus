## 1. What changed

Before: a photo proposal (T-056) showed only "SAM 3.1 · N objects". The boxes went into the vision prompt and were not kept, and no API returned the stored photo.

After: on the page of a proposal asked for with a photo, a card **The photo** shows the stored photo with every box SAM 3.1 found drawn over it at the photo's own pixels, each with its number and (on a box that is not thin, at 720 px and wider) its concept, and the same objects as a numbered list with their confidence ("smt line, confidence 93%") for screen readers and narrow screens.

## 7. Risks

- Boxes are kept only for photo proposals generated after this change; proposals already in the deployment show the photo alone (as asked: never an error).
- A real photo with many overlapping objects (up to 64) will crowd the labels; the numbered list stays readable, and the labels never widen the page (the frame clips them).
- The Accent Fill stroke is chosen for the synthetic floor plan; on a very dark or violet photo the boxes are harder to see.
- The image is fetched and held in memory as a Blob (at most 5 MiB) once per page view; no cache by design.
- `next dev`/`next build` with Turbopack cannot follow a symlinked `node_modules` (this worktree borrowed the main checkout's); the browser look was checked with `next dev --webpack`. CI installs its own, so this does not affect CI.
