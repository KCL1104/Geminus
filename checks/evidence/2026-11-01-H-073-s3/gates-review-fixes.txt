H-073 slice 3, the review's two findings (on a1c4321), 2026-11-01.

1. A fingerprint opened by hover could not be closed with Escape (WCAG 1.4.13)
   The chip opens over the rows below it. The only Escape handler was the
   span's onKeyDown, and a browser sends a key to the focused element, so a
   pointer resting on a fingerprint with the focus elsewhere could not close
   it. Also: Escape while focused and hovered, then the pointer leaving,
   cleared the dismissal and the chip opened again through :focus.
   Fix (apps/web/features/results/proof.tsx, Hash): while the chip shows on
   hover, a capture listener on the document takes Escape (not during IME
   composition), closes the chip and stops that Escape there; a dismissed
   chip stays hidden until neither the pointer nor the focus is on it.
   Tests first (apps/web/tests/results-proof.test.tsx):
     - "hides a value opened by hover on Escape, wherever the key goes, and
       lets other Escapes through"
     - "keeps a value hidden after Escape until neither the pointer nor the
       focus is on it"
   Before the fix: npx vitest run tests/results-proof.test.tsx
     Tests  4 failed | 20 passed (24)   (the two new tests, in both projects
     wonder-on and wonder-off; "expected false to be true" at the hover
     Escape and at the pointer leaving while focused)
   After the fix: Tests 24 passed (24).
   Mutation checks: dropping the document listener's stopPropagation fails
   the first new test; resetting the dismissal on pointer leave while focused
   fails the second. Both restored.
