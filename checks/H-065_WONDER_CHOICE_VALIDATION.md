## 7. Risks

1. **Spend**: a Muse Wonder call now holds about USD 6.9 to 11 of the day while it runs (a repair up to about USD 15) (up to 900 s, and its repair again), so under the USD 40 caps about four Muse Wonder calls can be reserved at once, and fewer once the day has spent; then `AI_QUOTA_EXCEEDED`. The USD 100 month still stops everything. A research reservation is still not a ceiling (H-046 (2)).
2. **Time**: a Muse Wonder proposal may take up to 15 minutes, 30 with its repair. The page keeps polling (every 1.5 s) and says it can take several minutes; nothing on the page shows a time limit. A worker restart (a Railway redeploy) during the call ends the attempt (`WORKER_STOPPED`), and the single-attempt job is not run again; the person asks again. Railway's stop grace period was not checked.
3. **Concurrency**: the per-organization limit of 2 calls in flight (`MaxConcurrentPerOrganization`) is held for the whole call, so one long Muse Wonder proposal halves what that organization can run at once on that worker.
4. **The answer's size**: the output bound grows, the bytes the server reads do not (`MaxOutputBytes` 256 KiB; Meta's body bound 8 MiB). Reasoning is not part of the answer text, so a proposal answer near 256 KiB is not expected, but one would be refused as before.
5. **Unit and browser time**: the unit suite now runs twice (about 70 s instead of about 60 s locally, in parallel); the browser checks build and run twice (CI's e2e job takes about twice as long).
6. **Merge**: the E1 branch changes `internal/ai/provider` too; this diff there is the bounds block of `call` and comments. A conflict there is in `provider.go` around the image and research bounds.
